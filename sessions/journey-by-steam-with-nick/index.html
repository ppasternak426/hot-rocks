What to expect

Close your eyes and picture a lemon grove in Sicily. There’s sage beneath the trees, Ennio Morricone is playing and a wave of warm steam passes over the bench. It’s one of six stops on this guided Aufguss evening, where Nick McEwen uses scent, music, breathwork and storytelling to give each destination a character of its own.
